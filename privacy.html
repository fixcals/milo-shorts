Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Milo Shorts Uploader (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is a private, internal tool that publishes videos to our own YouTube channel, Milo Shorts. It is not offered to the public: the only account that authorizes it is the owner of that channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

The app requests a single OAuth permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses it only to upload videos to our own channel and set their title, description, tags, category, audience and scheduled publication time. It does not read, collect or modify any other data from your Google Account or from other YouTube channels or users.

What we store

  • OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens), stored locally on the computer that runs the app, in a private configuration file that is never published or shared.
  • Information about our own uploads: the YouTube video ID, title, publication status and scheduled time of each video we publish, kept in a local database so that the app never uploads the same video twice.

We do not collect personal information from viewers or any other users. The app has no user accounts, no analytics and does not use cookies.

How we use and share information

The information above is used only to publish and schedule our own videos. We do not sell, rent or share it with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Upload records are kept while the channel is active. You can delete the stored credentials at any time by removing the local configuration file, and you can revoke the app’s access to your Google Account at any moment from the Google security settings page. After revocation the app can no longer access your account.

Security

Credentials are stored only on the machine that runs the app, are excluded from version control and are never written to logs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
