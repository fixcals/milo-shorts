MILO SHORTS

Football data that comes to life

Animated football statistics, one Short a day

Milo Shorts is a YouTube channel that publishes short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) showing how football statistics evolve over time: titles, goals, standings and records, animated as bar chart races and similar charts.

All the data comes from public sources, such as Wikipedia and RSSSF, and every video credits its source in the description. The animations are original and generated by our own code. We do not use third-party footage, club crests, league logos or unlicensed music.

Uses YouTube API Services

Milo Shorts Uploader

Milo Shorts Uploader is the private, internal tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) only to upload and schedule our own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.
